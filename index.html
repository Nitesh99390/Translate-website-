Novelxplin brings EPUB, PDF, Word documents and plain text into one reading workspace. Translate, check the chapters and export a book you can take with you.

Try English to Hindi translation →
  1. 01

    Open your document

    Choose an EPUB, text-based PDF, DOCX or TXT. Files are parsed in your browser; scanned PDFs need OCR elsewhere first.

  2. 02

    Choose how to translate

    Use Chrome’s page translator in this workspace, or Deep Translate for API-based translation in 30 languages.

  3. 03

    Review, export, keep reading

    Check flagged chapters and edit the text. Download an EPUB, TXT, chapter ZIP, Markdown or HTML file.
