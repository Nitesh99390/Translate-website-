Runs entirely in your browser

Verify every chapter of your
Chrome translation before you export

Upload an EPUB, PDF, DOCX or TXT. Turn on Chrome's built-in translate. The verifier walks through each chapter, confirms the text actually changed, and packages the result — nothing ever leaves your device.

Chapter-by-chapter verification

Each section is rendered, scrolled, and checked for a real script or text change before it's accepted.

Private by design

Parsing, verification and export all happen locally. Progress is auto-saved in your browser's IndexedDB.

Review & arrange

Rename, reorder with drag-and-drop, exclude chapters, and fix recurring mistranslations with find & replace.

Five export formats

Story TXT, per-chapter ZIP, EPUB3 with table of contents, Markdown, or a self-contained HTML page.

How it works
  1. 1

    Upload

    Pick or drop a file. The document is split into chapters, pages or sections.

  2. 2

    Enable Chrome translate

    Use the address-bar icon (or right-click → Translate) and pick your target language.

  3. 3

    Run & export

    Press S to start. Watch live progress, then download in the format you need.
