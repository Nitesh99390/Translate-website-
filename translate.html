Translate the whole book, directly.

Drop an EPUB, PDF, DOCX or TXT. Deep Translate splits it into small chunks, sends each one to a translation API, verifies the result and stitches the book back together — English → Hindi by default, 30 languages supported. Progress is saved after every chunk, so a dropped connection just picks up where it left off.
