Welcome to Xplin Go

Translate whole books on your phone in 3 easy steps.

  1. Open a book.epub · .pdf · .docx · .txt — or try the built-in sample.
  2. Turn on your browser's translateChrome ⋮ → Translate → choose your language.
  3. Tap ▶ StartThe book scrolls chapter by chapter and every chapter is verified before it's saved.

Files never leave your phone. Use the bottom bar for Chapters · Start · Export · Menu.
